TalkHub Account Deletion & Data Removal

At TalkHub, we respect your privacy and provide users with full control over their personal information. This page explains how to permanently delete your TalkHub account and associated data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the TalkHub application.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your request.

If you cannot access the application, you may request account deletion by contacting support.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User profile information
  • Email address
  • Uploaded posts and media
  • Comments and reactions
  • Chat messages
  • Authentication records
  • Associated account settings

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be temporarily retained when required for:

  • Legal obligations
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security investigations
  • System backup recovery

Any retained data is securely protected and removed according to applicable policies.

Deletion Timeline

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 days. Backup systems may take up to 30 days to fully remove deleted information.

Third-Party Services

TalkHub may use trusted third-party services such as:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
  • Cloudinary Media Storage
